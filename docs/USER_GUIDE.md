# Player quick start

Open the one-use invitation supplied by your DM, choose a unique username, display name and password, then Join the adventure. This creates an account on that DM's host, not a public service. Keep your credentials: recovery/rejoin tools are still being built. A used, expired or revoked link cannot create another account.

The host must remain running. On later visits, open its normal address and sign in with the same username/password. The campaign library lists only current memberships. Players cannot create campaigns or invitations, alter campaign settings, see the private sentinel or read the DM note/art.

The table supports custom scenes, live token movement and shared journal reading. The DM selects which scene is active for players; private preparation scenes are absent from your view. If the active scene is made private, your map clears until another is shown. Your DM assigns control in Scene → token → Controlled by. When the connection says Live, drag your token to snap to the grid. You can also select it in Scene and enter Token X/Y, or focus the map and use arrow keys. Unassigned tokens remain view-only. Drag the background to pan; scroll/+/- zoom and Fit scene are local to you.

Editing pauses while Connecting/Reconnecting. A fresh snapshot catches up after reconnect; your camera stays in place. A conflicting move shows an error: review the current position and try again. Characters shows shared sheets and those explicitly granted to you. Observers read; owners edit ordinary fields and move linked shared tokens. Private DM notes are never sent. Open sheet on a linked token opens its character. Concurrent saves require Reload latest sheet, which replaces unsaved values. Chat/private whispers are available in Chat; Combat is available for manual initiative and linked HP; automated system rules arrive later. Background image bytes are downloadable by authorized viewers; protected background confidentiality is not implemented.

If your access is revoked, the current view closes when the live connection is reauthorized; direct requests are denied immediately. Contact the DM for account problems. If the host cannot be reached, check that the process is running, the network address is correct and both devices are on the intended trusted network.

Shared manual fog is controlled by your DM. A shared token appears only when its whole footprint is revealed, and you can move only to a fully revealed destination. Covering it again removes it from your canvas and scene list. The whole map image is still downloadable. Actor-sheet grants remain independent of fog; your DM may explicitly share a sheet while its token is covered.

Dice now supports integers, NdM/dM, parentheses, unary signs and +,-,*,/. Choose visibility before rolling; quick dice keep that choice. Public reaches all current members, DM + me reaches you and the DM, Only me reaches only your account, Blind reaches the DM and gives you an acceptance message. Other DMs do not receive a player's self-only result through the app. The host/database owner remains trusted. Recent history contains only results you may read and survives reload/reconnect. The host retains1000 rolls per campaign and displays your latest20. Invalid/too-fast rolls show an error without inventing or accepting a result. Selected participants reaches the roller and chosen current members, including DM only when chosen. Advanced notation remains future work.

Journal shows currently shared DM-authored pages. Sharing, concealment and deletion update live and after reconnect. Pages are literal plain text; player editing, rich formatting and private player notes remain future work.

Chat supports public messages and private group whispers to1–8 selected current members. The sender receives its own messages; include DM explicitly if needed. Selected dice follows the same rule. Roll cards appear alongside text, with privacy/time labels. Drafts and selections survive dock tabs; disconnected/pending/archived states pause sending. A removed recipient must be corrected before retry. Reconnect restores current authorized history; revocation closes the table. Plain text only, up to1000 characters; counter counts Unicode scalars. Participant directory is membership rather than online presence.

Repeated display names include a distinguishing account-ID label in recipients and conversation authors, so you can select the intended account and identify replies. Login usernames remain private.

The table now uses a compact VTT workspace. Scene tabs are above the map; Select/Pan/tools are on the left; Chat, Combat, Characters, Journal, Dice and Scene live in the right sidebar. Collapse/expand and drag its divider, or focus the divider and use Left/Right to resize. On small screens the sidebar overlays the table. Sidebar changes keep camera and in-memory private drafts. Quick dice at the bottom prepare a formula, open Dice and retain the chosen scope; they do not roll automatically. Shift+C/D/I/J opens Chat/Dice/Combat/Journal; Shift+B toggles the sidebar. Shortcuts ignore text fields and open dialogs. Help lists controls.

Combat shows visible participants in the DM's encounter. Your controlled token can set its manual initiative; linked character owners can edit HP. Other fields remain read-only. Hidden turns display Awaiting GM without disclosing the combatant. The DM advances turns. Changed-elsewhere drafts require explicit field reload. Actor-sheet grants are independent of token fog. The member strip lists membership, not online presence.
